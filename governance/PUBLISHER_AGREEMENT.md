## Agreement

By requesting trusted publisher status, the publisher confirms:

1. The publisher has the right to submit and license all contributions it
   submits to AXIS-Library.
2. Contributions are licensed under Apache-2.0 unless explicitly accepted under
   another compatible license by maintainers.
3. The publisher will not knowingly submit private, proprietary, trade-secret,
   or license-incompatible code.
4. The publisher is responsible for reviewing AI-generated code before
   submission.
5. The publisher will disclose material AI assistance in submissions.
6. The publisher will sign trusted entries with the accepted publisher key.
7. The publisher will promptly report incorrect, insecure, compromised, or
   policy-violating entries.
8. The publisher accepts that bad immutable IDs are handled through signed
   revocation feeds and replacement IDs, not silent mutation.
9. The publisher accepts removal from `trust/trust.json` if its key is
   compromised or if it violates marketplace policy.

## Maintainer Acceptance

Trusted publisher status is active only after maintainers:

- review this agreement,
- review the publisher application,
- add the publisher key to `trust/trust.json`,
- merge the change through CI.
